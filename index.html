How do I use the generated CSS?

Copy the output and paste it as the box-shadow property on any element in your stylesheet.

Can I stack multiple shadows?

This generator outputs a single shadow layer. To stack shadows, generate each one separately and combine them with a comma in your CSS: box-shadow: shadow1, shadow2;

What's the difference between blur and spread?

Blur softens the shadow's edges — higher values fade out further. Spread expands or shrinks the shadow's size before blurring is applied, independent of the element's own size.
